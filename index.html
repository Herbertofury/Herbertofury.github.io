Connected accounts

Your Google account, connected on your device.

Connect Google when you want to download files you can access in Drive, import catalogue research from Sheets or Docs, or create and update research exports. Enderloom stores its Google connection encrypted in your local app profile and shares it across those features.

You choose the files and actions. Website sign-ins use the app’s persistent browser session; Google API permissions use a separate consent grant. You can disconnect Google from Enderloom and revoke access through your Google Account.
