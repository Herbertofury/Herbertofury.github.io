Privacy policy

Effective September 23, 2026. This policy describes the Enderloom desktop application and this informational website, maintained through the Enderloom project.

Data used by the app

Enderloom works with files and projects you select, Minecraft instance metadata, catalogue entries, provider information, downloaded files, local history and diagnostic logs. Its browser stores website data, including sign-in cookies, in the local application profile. Websites opened in the app receive normal browser requests and are governed by their own privacy policies.

Google account access

When you connect Google, Enderloom receives your account identity, email address and profile information, along with the permissions you approve. Its Google features use these permissions:

  • Drive read access: locate, read, export and download Drive files you choose or reference in a project or catalogue.
  • Drive file access: create and manage files created or opened through Enderloom’s supported export workflows.
  • Sheets and Docs access: read research and catalogue sources, and create or update exports when you request those actions.

These permissions may allow broader access than an individual action needs. Connecting an account does not itself instruct Enderloom to import every file, edit existing documents or share them publicly.

Storage and protection

Google refresh credentials are encrypted using the operating system’s secure storage through Electron and kept in your local app profile. Access tokens are used by Enderloom’s main process for requests to Google APIs. Project data, imported content, browser storage, downloads and diagnostic records are stored locally. Encryption of the account credential does not mean all downloaded files or browser data have the same protection.

Enderloom sends the data necessary to perform your requested actions to the relevant provider. Google APIs, GitHub, CurseForge, Modrinth and websites you visit operate under their own terms. If you choose to export, upload, share, or use another integration, the destination may receive the content you send. Local logs and exported evidence can contain filenames, project paths or provider details.

Use and sharing of Google user data

Google user data is used to provide the connected download, catalogue, research and export features you request. Enderloom does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train general-purpose AI models. Enderloom’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

The Enderloom maintainer does not receive your Google connection or private documents merely because you connect the app. Sharing a diagnostic report or project archive with another person is a separate action that may disclose its contents.

Retention and your controls

Enderloom retains local project data and sign-ins so they can survive app restarts. Disconnect Google in Enderloom to remove its saved OAuth credential. You can also revoke the grant using Google Account connections. Google may expire or revoke grants independently.

Disconnecting the API connection does not delete downloaded files, imported catalogue content or browser cookies. Remove those separately using the relevant app, website or filesystem controls. Delete local project copies or exports you no longer want to retain. Files saved to Google remain in your Google account until you remove them there.

This website

This static site does not add analytics, advertising scripts or its own tracking cookies. It is hosted by GitHub Pages; hosting requests are subject to GitHub’s privacy statement.

Questions and changes

Contact the maintainer through Enderloom support. Do not post credentials or private documents in a public issue. Updates to this policy will appear here with a revised effective date.
